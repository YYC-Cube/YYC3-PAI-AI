# 贡献者公约 | Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 承诺 | Our Pledge

我们作为成员、贡献者和维护者承诺：让本社区的参与体验对所有人都是无骚扰的——无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人形象、种族、宗教或性取向。
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 准则 | Our Standards

**鼓励的行为 | Examples of positive behavior:**

- 对他人展现同理心与善意 | Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- 尊重不同意见、观点与经验 | Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- 给予并优雅接受建设性反馈 | Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- 承担责任、向受影响者致歉并从中学习 | Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- 以社区利益为先，而非仅顾个人 | Focusing on what is best for the overall community

**不可接受的行为 | Unacceptable behavior:**

- 性化语言/图像及任何形式的骚扰 | Sexualized language/imagery and harassment of any kind
- 恶意侮辱、贬损性评论或人身/政治攻击 | Trolling, insulting comments, personal or political attacks
- 公开或私下骚扰 | Public or private harassment
- 未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息 | Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- 其它在专业场合可合理认定不当的行为 | Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## 职责 | Enforcement Responsibilities

社区维护者有责任解释并执行本公约，对可接受的行为做出公平、适当的纠正行动，并对本页中任何被认为不当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为采取澄清与处置措施（包括警告、临时或永久封禁）。
Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair, appropriate corrective action in response to any unacceptable behavior (including warnings, temporary or permanent bans).

## 范围 | Scope

本公约适用于所有社区空间（仓库、Issue、PR、聊天群、线下活动），也适用于在公共空间中代表本项目的个人行为。
This Code applies within all community spaces (repo, issues, PRs, chat, events) and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## 执行 | Enforcement

违例行为请通过 **admin@yanyucloud.com** 举报；所有投诉都会被及时、公正地审查与调查，并尊重事件各方的隐私与安全。
Report issues via **admin@yanyucloud.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with respect for the privacy and security of the reporter.

执行记录与裁定说明将按需向社区公示（脱敏处理）。
Enforcement outcomes will be published (redacted) when appropriate.
